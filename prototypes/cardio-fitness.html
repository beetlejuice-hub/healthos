Cardio fitness, nothing typed

Your band already counts steps every 5 minutes. Steps a minute say how hard you're going, so the app can compare your heart rate at the same step rate week after week. If it's lower at the same pace, you're fitter. That works three ways: daily walking (100–130 a minute, outside workouts), running (140+ or inside a Run) and the stairmaster (inside a Stairmaster session). On top of that, how fast your heart rate drops in the 2 minutes after cardio, plus resting heart rate and VO2 max on the same weeks. The numbers below are made-up examples with a known change built in.

1 · The Worker keeps each 5-minute window my pick

  • For every 5 minutes with 40+ steps a minute: the time, steps a minute, the average heart rate. About 1 kB a day.
  • Worked out on each pull, and once over the 120 days the Worker holds now. That's a trend back to mid-July from day one.
  • Kept 2 years, so long trends outlive the 120 days of minutes.
  • Recovery after cardio is worked out from the minutes the Worker holds. The app just asks for it.

2 · Save a summary on each workout not this

  • Each session's heart rate at its step rate and its recovery are saved on the workout, with a one-time backfill through older weeks.
  • Daily walking isn't a session, so it would be left out entirely. That's the lane with the most data.
  • Recomputing after a fix means rewriting every workout.
