    How it's worked out

    Caffeine isn't in you the moment you drink it. It moves from your gut into your blood over 30–60 minutes (faster from a shot, slower with food or when you sip a can), then leaves at a steady half-life.

    Half-life differs a lot between people, so the faint band shows the range most adults fall in (). Once the wearable sees how you sleep, it can learn yours and the band narrows.

    "Until" times keep you at or under 50 mg for the first three hours of sleep, when the deepest sleep happens. That catches a late coffee that's barely absorbed at lights-out but peaks after you fall asleep.
