How Balance works

  1. Every day, real weight moves by (eaten − burned) ÷ 7,700. Water sits on top of it, comes and goes, and the scale adds its own bounce.
  2. A filter walks through your days and keeps a best guess, with error bars, of real weight, water and burn. Then it walks back so early days use later evidence too.
  3. Unlogged days aren't assumed average. What they add is a separate number, learned from the weight they caused.
  4. Water size, how fast it settles, and whether your burn drifts or stepped on some day aren't settings. Each option is tried, and the answers are blended by how well each explains your scale.
