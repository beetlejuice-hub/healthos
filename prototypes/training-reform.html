Training: am I getting fitter, and what next

Today Training is ten cards of data, and you have to put the story together yourself. The renovation makes the top answer one question across strength, cardio fitness, recovery and weight, all on the same 12 weeks, and then say what to do next. The cards you like stay below it, in the same order as the question. Three directions for the top. The numbers are made-up examples.

A · Four tiles, then nextThe four answers as tiles, each tile on the same 12 weeks. Tap one to jump to its card. Then "Next": the session after this one and the cardio you're short this week, each with Start. Clickable.
B · One timelineAll four on one chart, each as change since the start, so up is always better. Your sessions are ticks along the bottom: lifting in yellow, cardio in green. Tap a legend item to hide that line.
C · The paragraphThe 12 weeks written out in two sentences, with the numbers in them, then next steps as a list with buttons. The most "just tell me" of the three.

The page, top to bottom (any direction)

Answer and next
  1. Getting fitter? A, B or C new
  2. Next: the next session's first lift, cardio short this week new
  3. This week: sessions, working sets smaller
The detail, in the question's order
  1. Strength: overall line, your 4 lifts, recent sessions, muscles
  2. Cardio: minutes a week, pace and distance
  3. Cardio fitness: walking, stairmaster, running, recovery
  4. Weight: the trend line from Food & body, small here too
Folded, one tap away
  1. What goes with a strong session folded
  2. What training goes with afterwards folded
  3. Muscle strength rows merged into the muscles card merged

My pick is A: the four answers are visible without reading, and each tile leads somewhere. B is the prettiest, but four lines on one phone chart get busy. C works as the line under A's tiles. Workout's home screen gets the same Next card.
